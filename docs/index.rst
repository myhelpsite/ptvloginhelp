Common Login Issues and Solutions
--------------------------------

While logging into Peacock TV is usually smooth, some users may face issues. Here are common problems and how to fix them.

Incorrect email or password is one of the most frequent issues. Double-check your credentials and ensure that the caps lock is turned off while entering your password.

If you have forgotten your password, use the password recovery option available on the login page. Follow the instructions to reset your password and regain access to your account.

Sometimes, users experience login errors due to poor internet connectivity. Ensure that your device is connected to a stable internet network before attempting to log in.

Clearing your browser cache or app data can also resolve login problems. This helps remove temporary files that might be causing conflicts.

Account-related issues such as expired subscriptions or account suspension may also prevent login. In such cases, verify your subscription status or contact customer support for assistance.
